THE TECHNICAL PROBLEM IN ONE PICTURE
Thermal Expansion Discontinuity
Metallic base ( alpha/approx 6.44-14.0 \mu m/mK) vs LZO coating (alpha = 9.1 \mu m/mK). Interface shear forces exceed 850 MPa, far surpassing the thermal limits of legacy Inconel 718 and CMSX-4.
Microstructural Phase Decay
Oxygen diffusion precipitates brittle sigma phases ($VEC \ge 6.8$), causing rapid material loss and interface failure.
The Cooling Hole Threat
Laser-drilled holes create stress concentration ($K_t \ge 4.5$), accelerating crack propagation and layer delamination.
The Unified Imperative
Substrate, cooling channels, and insulating layers must be optimized as a single system to prevent failure.

VALIDATION PLAN TO TRL 4
6-Month Rapid Co-Validation Sprint & Milestone Framework
PHASE 1
MONTHS 1–2
Multi-Physics Structural Audits
Execution of unreduced 3D FEniCS FEA stress field grids and MACE Molecular Dynamics runs via external cloud supercomputing to confirm a structural Safety Factor ≥ 1.50.
PHASE 2
MONTHS 3–4
Additive Prototyping & Porosity Programming
Feeding the universal reference matrix into a dual-line multi-hopper LENS system. Optimizing laser power and powder flow to program the integrated transpiration micro-pores.
PHASE 3
MONTHS 5–6
High-Temperature Characterization
Exposure of fabricated coupons to 1,000 continuous thermal shock cycles inside an automated Jet Burner Rig at a real gas temperature of 1400°C (1673 K).
Go/No-Go Gatekeeping Criteria
Microstructural characterization via SEM/EDS must confirm clear layer-wise compositional layout and verify the complete absence of processing voids or macro-cracking before advancing to full turbine blade geometries.
