THE TECHNICAL PROBLEM IN ONE PICTURE
Thermal Expansion Discontinuity
Metallic base (α ≈ 6.44–14.0 μm/m·K) vs LZO coating (α = 9.1 μm/m·K). Interface shear forces exceed 850 MPa, far surpassing the thermal limits of legacy Inconel 718 and CMSX-4.
Microstructural Phase Decay
Oxygen diffusion precipitates brittle sigma phases , causing rapid material loss and interface failure.
The Cooling Hole Threat
Laser-drilled holes create stress concentration , accelerating crack propagation and layer delamination.
The Unified Imperative
Substrate, cooling channels, and insulating layers must be optimized as a single system to prevent failure.
