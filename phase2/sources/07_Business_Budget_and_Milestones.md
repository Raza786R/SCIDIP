# Business case budget and delivery milestones
Crystal Paradigm | A techno commercial proposal for SCIDIP preparation

Our commercial hypothesis is that a traceable materials selection and validation service can reduce the cost and uncertainty of a defined development decision. We do not yet have measured discovery acceleration, a confirmed paying customer for HE MCGA, a validated industrial material or proven licensing revenue. The proposed INR 65 lakh program should fund that de risking, not assume it has already happened.

## Customer problem and first offer
Sona Comstar publicly describes precision warm and hot forged driveline products. A potential discussion is a surface or coupon study related to forging tool durability. That is our inference from its manufacturing activities, not a known internal die life problem or agreed collaboration. [S2]

First confirm the dominant failure mode and incumbent treatment. Thermal checking, abrasive wear, plastic collapse and adhesion failure can require different materials. The current screen has no contact pressure, friction or wear model and does not include a real tool steel substrate. A ceramic rich thermal barrier might be unsuitable for a wear surface. The pilot must be able to reject this architecture.

The first commercial deliverable should be a bounded feasibility study: requirement sheet, baseline, candidate rationale, manufacturability review, coupon data and go or no go report. A platform subscription or composition license is a later hypothesis if repeatable validated delivery and appropriate IP or data rights support it. Do not describe company scale, market size, customer conversion or prices as measured without evidence.

## Explicit economic scenario
The existing tool consumption calculator uses annual parts divided by life per tool, multiplied by tool cost. With one million parts per year, baseline life 50000 parts and cost INR 100000 per tool, annual modeled tooling cost is INR 20 lakh. Assuming life 65000 parts and cost INR 115000 gives about INR 17.69 lakh. A hypothetical INR 5 lakh pilot then has simple payback of about 2.17 years on the INR 2.31 lakh annual difference.

Every input is a scenario assumption, not a quotation or predicted life improvement. A 15 percent tool price premium requires more than 15 percent life improvement merely to lower this modeled tool expense. Downtime, scrap, quality, maintenance, qualification, tax and financing are excluded. If savings are nonpositive, the calculator returns no positive payback. The key future metric is cost per accepted part at equivalent performance.

Milestone one reviews requirements, benchmark, thermodynamic and process feasibility and an experimental protocol. Milestone two demonstrates a repeatable coupon route with verified chemistry, phase and defect records, or triggers a justified pivot. Milestone three evaluates thermal, environmental and relevant mechanical behavior against controls. Milestone four demonstrates one prospective measured feedback iteration and an application go or no go decision with cost evidence.

Numerical acceptance limits must be agreed before tests with the application owner and lab. Do not promise confirm safety factor 1.71, zero sigma phase or zero spallation as already established premises. The proposed gates should reveal whether the technology works, including negative decisions.

The phase expenditure allocations sum to INR 65 lakh and reconcile by budget head. They are not approved grant tranches. The website's approximate release percentages total 95 percent, while earlier proposal and guideline versions differ. ## Team and execution evidence
Assign one accountable technical lead, a materials and manufacturing owner, a computational owner and a finance and customer owner. Individuals may cover multiple roles if their capacity is credible. The earlier named biographies and credentials are supplied claims; verify current affiliations, consent, time allocation and this project's relevant capability before presenting them as committed execution resources.

The program page includes leader qualification and startup eligibility conditions. Confirm postgraduate technical qualification, company registration and ownership, incorporation age, cash inflow history and full time obligations against the applicable documents. These are diligence items, not verified facts supplied by this pack. [S1]

## What the panel needs to believe
The technical case needs an honest problem, executable method and evidence generating plan. Entrepreneurial intent needs a defined customer decision, ownership of execution and willingness to pivot. IP needs a plausible protectable contribution and accurate status. Team needs named capability and access. Business needs feasible milestones, quotations, cash planning and a credible route to value. These are the program's stated evaluation dimensions, not a claim that a favorable score is guaranteed. [S1]

Carry a finance presenter who can reconcile every number, explain assumptions and distinguish grant expenditure from future revenue. Keep quotations, eligibility documents, partner letters and data ownership terms available as supporting evidence rather than burying unsupported assertions in technical slides.
