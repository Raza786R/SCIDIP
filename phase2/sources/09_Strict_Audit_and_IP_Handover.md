# Strict project audit and IP preparation handover
Crystal Paradigm | What was corrected and what can still cause rejection

The project is now an auditable computational screening system. It is not yet a validated extreme environment material or a demonstrated commercial solution. This second review checks the Phase 1 repairs and corrects remaining operational defects before producing the teaching pack. A serious proposal should expose unresolved risks and show how the next evidence changes a decision.

## Historical claims that must be reconciled
The submitted material describes a 3D safety factor of 1.71, 798 MPa von Mises stress, oxidation mass gain 7.5 mg per square centimetre, extensive thermal cycles, zero sigma phase risk, cost reduction and assured manufacturing readiness. No corresponding verified target outputs sufficient to substantiate those claims were found in the executable evidence. Preserve them as historical assertions and use the current evidence qualified language when preparing the final deck.

The old claim that IIT Delhi has the only suitable Indian LENS cluster is not established. A facility listing is not access approval. The old SRM stator application lacks magnetic property and loss data. Program shortlisting is evidence of passing a selection process, not independent scientific validation of all performance assertions.

A claim reconciliation CSV accompanies the pack. It maps historical wording to current status and the evidence needed to promote a future claim. It should be consulted before copying a number from an older appendix or briefing.

## Invention disclosure preparation for the next phase
The next patent focused phase should start with an invention disclosure and a focused prior art search, not a claim that using AI, DE or eleven layers is inherently new. Potential subjects to investigate include a specific supported composition window, a manufacturable graded architecture, a validated process or a defined technical selection method producing a demonstrated effect. Current candidate novelty and inventiveness are not established.

Collect the contributors and their actual technical contributions, employment and assignment records, dated design and experiment history, exact embodiments, alternatives, comparative results, reproducibility information, public disclosure dates and intended commercial use. For each potential contribution, record what is new relative to known work and which evidence supports its technical effect. Do not invent an advantage simply to fill a patent section.

## Final deck preparation boundary
The following deck phase should lead with the problem, the method and the current evidence, then ask for support to resolve a defined uncertainty. Use a small main story with technical backup, a reconciled finance sheet and clearly proposed milestones. Do not silently replace an unverified performance claim with another attractive number from a different model or candidate.

Before pitching, confirm eligibility, named team participation, facility feasibility, cost quotations and the latest event email. Before any public release, review contributor consent, data rights and disclosure implications. The goal of this pack is that every team member can explain both the scientific mechanism and the precise limits of what we have done.
