# Validation and IIT Delhi collaboration plan
Crystal Paradigm | Experiments facilities and decisions for a research partnership

The collaboration request should be a staged de risking program. Our software supplies traceable candidate proposals and a model whose assumptions are explicit. We need complementary thermodynamics, manufacturing, characterization and application expertise. No faculty member, instrument booking or sponsor pilot is currently confirmed by this documentation.

## Decide what a successful proof of concept means
Choose one primary application and one incumbent baseline before testing. Agree the service environment, failure mechanism, specimen geometry, process route and acceptance criteria. Aerospace remains a long term research motivation; a forging tool surface coupon is a separate nearer term discovery hypothesis that still needs contact and wear requirements.

A useful initial study compares an incumbent, a sharp or ungraded control and a graded proposal under the same protocol. Use independently manufactured replicates; three per condition is an initial screening proposal, not a qualification sample size. Let variability and the application's reliability need determine the final design. Multiple readings from one specimen are not independent manufacture repeats.

## Experiments arranged by question
| Question | Evidence and interpretation |
| What did we make | Actual chemistry, density, porosity, dimensions and process history |
| Which phases exist | XRD with suitable analysis plus microscopy; repeat after thermal exposure |
| Are elements distributed as designed | SEM EDS cross sections; account for sampling and analytical limits |
| Are thermal assumptions reasonable | CTE, conductivity or diffusivity with heat capacity and density, and modulus over relevant temperatures |
| Does the environment damage it | Controlled oxidation or corrosion history with area, atmosphere, mass change and scale examination |
| Is the bond durable | Route appropriate adhesion or fracture assessment, crack and reaction layer analysis |
| Does it support the intended load | Relevant compression and separate tensile or toughness evidence; creep and fatigue where needed |
| Does the application benefit | Controlled cycling, wear or contact tests against the incumbent at equal part quality |

XRD means X ray diffraction; SEM means scanning electron microscopy; EDS means energy dispersive X ray spectroscopy. DSC is differential scanning calorimetry and TGA is thermogravimetric analysis. CTE is the coefficient of thermal expansion.

XRD identifies crystalline phase information within detection and analysis limits; it does not prove a perfect absence of a phase at every length scale. SEM provides morphology and defect information; EDS gives elemental distributions, not a complete crystallographic or bonding explanation. TGA tracks mass change under a controlled temperature and atmosphere history; it is not an automated coating life or safety factor certificate.

A materials lead should own chemistry and phase plausibility with a thermodynamics specialist where needed. A process partner should own safe fabrication feasibility and recorded process control. A characterization partner should own methods, uncertainties and raw data. Sona engineers would ideally define a use case, incumbent and industrial acceptance criteria if they choose to participate.

Separate team biographies supplied in earlier documents from verified employment, participation and capability for this particular work.

## Decision gates over twenty four months
Gate one is a reviewed application and experimental plan with resolved data and process risks. Gate two is a reproducible coupon process with verified chemistry and phase information, or a documented pivot if manufacture is not viable. Gate three is a comparison of environmental, thermal and mechanical behavior against a baseline. Gate four is a prospective feedback iteration and application feasibility decision with an auditable data package.

A gate can fail. Agree what result triggers a change of alloy, ceramic, route or application. Avoid milestones such as confirm the already achieved safety factor, which assume the desired conclusion. Budget should fund decisions and evidence, not a guaranteed positive number.

## How model validation differs from verification
Verification asks whether the code solves its stated equations consistently; our analytical and conservation checks address this. Validation asks whether those equations and properties reproduce relevant observations. Calibration estimates parameters from selected data. Independent validation tests predictions without fitting to those outcomes. Qualification is a further application specific process; it is not automatically earned by a coupon score or a TRL label.

A larger finite element mesh, more cloud compute or an atomistic technique name cannot replace missing material state and constitutive data. Start with measured constants and an identifiable experiment. Progress to substrate, bond, transient, creep and fracture models as the evidence justifies them.
